Parade guide

Class movement, costume preparation and practical reminders for teachers supporting the parade.

YOUR PARADE PLAN

Three parade-day priorities

Stay with your class

Follow your teacher and class leader. Let them know if you need a break.

Be kind & make space

Walk calmly, be gentle with other people’s costumes and look out for each other.

Keep comfortable

Bring your water bottle and tell an adult if you feel tired or unwell.

Route & meeting points will be confirmed

The 2026 parade route, class order and assembly points have not yet been supplied. Your teacher or event team will share the final directions. Check classroom guidance.

These are general family preparation tips. Final event mechanics and safety instructions will come from the organizing team.

DRESS WITH JOY

A costume with a story

Your costume helps you tell the story of your assigned saint. It can be simple, thoughtful and comfortable.

  • Confirm your assigned saint with your teacher before making a costume.
  • Ask a parent or guardian to help. Choose clothes and shoes you can walk in easily.
  • Keep props light and safe. Ask your teacher about any prop restrictions.
  • Help students learn a fact about their saint and the virtue they represent.
Meet the saints

Safety & supervision

If you can’t find your group, stop and ask a teacher or event volunteer for help. Stay inside the parish event area and tell a trusted adult where you are.

If you feel unwell or need urgent help, tell a nearby responsible adult immediately.

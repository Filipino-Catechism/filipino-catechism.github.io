LET’S MAKE THINGS SIMPLE

Frequently asked questions

When and where is the parade?

Friday, 6 November 2026 at the Parish Grounds, Our Lady of the Rosary Parish, Doha, Qatar. Arrival time and the final schedule are awaiting confirmation.

How do I find my assigned saint?

Ask your catechism teacher to confirm your 2026 saint assignment. The saint discovery cards on this website are general learning stories, not personal assignments.

How do I find my classroom?

Your teacher will share your confirmed room and meeting point. The 2026 room list has not yet been supplied. Don’t use last year’s room assignments as current instructions.

Do I need an elaborate costume?

Keep it thoughtful, comfortable and easy to walk in. Confirm your saint and any costume requirements with your teacher before preparing it.

What should I bring?

Your saint costume, a water bottle, and the name tag and materials your teacher requests. The packing checklist can help you prepare.

Are the linked documents for 2026?

Documents marked 2025 reference come from the previous Canva event. Available planning PDFs can be read and downloaded here. Other files remain with their owners and may require sign-in. The team must approve current 2026 versions.

How will I receive a participation certificate?

The 2026 certificate and issuing process are awaiting confirmation. The original Canva certificate is available in the teacher resource library.

Does this site collect my child’s information?

This site does not collect student names, contact details, assignments or photos. Preparation checklist progress is saved only in your browser on this device. External documents remain subject to their owners’ access permissions.

Mechanics & Guidelines

Entries must depict your class’s assigned saint, their symbols and stories. Everyone in class can participate and submit. The catechist will choose the top three artworks.

Format & materials

  • A4 paper, portrait orientation.
  • Pencil, coloured pencil, crayons or watercolour.
  • Digital art is not allowed.

Age categories

  • Category 1: KG1, KG2, Level 1 & Level 2.
  • Category 2: Levels 3, 4, 5 & 6.
  • Category 3: Levels 7, 8, 9 & 10.

Rules

  • Write your name, level and section at the back of your drawing.
  • Be original: no copying or tracing. Use your own interpretation.
  • Stay on theme: clearly connect the artwork to the saint and their symbols.
  • Present it neatly: no folds, smudges or distracting borders.

Class Guidelines

➢All members of the class should participate

  • Catechists/Students can come up on how they would dress up during the parade
    Examples:
    • All will dress in a particular color
    • They can come dressed as nuns and priests
    • They can dress as Angels
    • They can wear their class t-shirts (if any)
    • They can also dress up in various saint costumes (although it will only be for the parade and not part of the official competition)

➢The Class has to prepare a Class Banner/Placard displaying:

  • Name of the Saint
  • Patron saint of / what the saint is known for
  • One-line attribute, virtue, or quotation associated with the saint

Banner/Placard – any size and be creative.

Class Representative Guidelines

Each section has one pair of representatives: one male and one female, portraying their assigned saints.

Submit class representative names by Friday, 23 October 2026.

Each representative must wear a sash bearing the saint’s name.

The Committee will provide the Sash Ribbons. Each Class Catechist will prepare the lettering based on their preferred design, either printed or customized.

Each representative must prepare a short introduction of 30–40 seconds maximum. It may be read from notes or delivered from memory.

The introduction may include

  • The saint’s name and patronage, or what they are known for.
  • A key virtue or characteristic.
  • A meaningful quote.
  • A brief explanation of the symbols or props used in the portrayal.

Important Reminder

Families are encouraged to create costumes with simplicity, creativity and care. Avoid costly or tailor-made outfits and use materials available at home whenever possible.

Let this activity teach frugality, humility, creativity and joyful simplicity, following the saints’ example. Participants are judged on saintly and joyful behaviour, knowledge, portrayal, creativity and simplicity—not on the cost or elaborateness of a costume.

PREPARE TOGETHER

To-do list & responsibilities

  1. To do

    Opening and closing

    Arrange the opening prayer, welcome remarks, introduction of the theme and closing prayer or remarks. Confirm who will deliver each part.

  2. To do

    Parade and saint presentations

    Plan how the children’s parade and saint or costume presentations fit into the program. Coordinate the sequence with the parade and emcee teams.

  3. To do

    Awarding and group photo

    Include prize awarding and the group photo in the program. Coordinate the handover with the judges, awards and documentation teams.

  4. To do

    Timeline and backup activities

    Prepare the program timeline and suitable backup activities in case of delays. Share the agreed sequence with the emcees and committee leads.

  5. To do

    Microphone, speakers and music

    Confirm the microphone and speaker requirements and prepare the music playlist with the logistics team before the celebration.

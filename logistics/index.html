Parade Winners & Awards

First, second and third place Best Saints (male and female) will be selected for every group: 12 winners per batch, 24 winners in total.

  • Batch 1: Group A and Group B — first, second and third place, male and female.
  • Batch 2: Group C and Group D — first, second and third place, male and female.

Special Awards

Four special awards in each batch — eight winners in total.

  • Most Creative Costume
  • Best Stage Presence
  • Most Joyful Participant
  • Parishioners Choice Award

Best Class Award: One class will be chosen in each batch.

Before You Arrive

  • Remind the children to eat lunch and hydrate before coming to class.
  • Please arrive on time so the celebration can begin on schedule.
  • Make sure everyone knows their classroom assignment, especially sections moved from their usual classrooms.
  • Ask children to bring only a light, small bag, or no bag if possible.
